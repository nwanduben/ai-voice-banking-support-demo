# Cards and Mobile/Internet Banking

## Why a card payment can be declined
- Not enough available funds.
- Online or international payments switched off. Switch them on in the ENTIN app under Cards.
- Daily card limit reached.
- Card expired or blocked.
- The payment was stopped for a security review. A specialist will help.

## Lost or stolen card
Block it immediately: in the app under Cards, or through the assistant. A blocked card can't be unblocked. A replacement can be collected at a branch, usually within 5 working days (fictional ENTIN timeline).

## Forgot PIN
ENTIN will never ask for your PIN. To set a new one, use "Change PIN" in the ENTIN app, or any ENTIN ATM with your card and a one-time code sent to your phone. Never tell the code to anyone.

## Forgot password / reset password
In the ENTIN app, tap "Forgot password" and follow the steps. You'll need your card or account details and a one-time code sent to your registered phone. Never share that code with anyone, including ENTIN staff.

## Profile locked
After 5 wrong login attempts your profile is locked for your protection. Reset it yourself using "Forgot password" in the app, or visit any branch with a valid ID. The assistant cannot unlock profiles.

## New phone / can't log in on a new device
For your security, the app must be linked to each new phone. Log in, then follow the "Link this device" steps.

## OTP not received
- Check your network signal and that your registered number is correct.
- Do-Not-Disturb (DND) on your line can block bank messages. Contact your network provider to allow transactional messages.
- You can switch OTP delivery to email in the app.
- Wait a few minutes before requesting a new code.
- Never share an OTP with anyone.
