# ENTIN Bank — About and Services (FICTIONAL DEMO BANK)

ENTIN Bank is a fictional Nigerian retail bank created to demonstrate an AI voice support system. It is not a real bank. No real accounts, cards or payments exist.

## Contact channels (fictional)
- Voice assistant (Ada): 24 hours, every day, on the ENTIN website.
- Human support: Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm WAT. Fraud and card blocking: 24 hours.
- USSD banking: dial *000*1# (fictional code).
- ENTIN mobile app: transfers, card controls (block, unblock, enable online payments), statements, change PIN, reset password.
- Email: support@entin-bank.example (fictional).

## Branch hours
- Most branches: Monday to Friday, 8am to 4pm.
- Selected branches (such as Lekki) also open Saturday, 10am to 2pm.
- Branches are closed on public holidays.
- Use the location tool to find a specific branch or ATM.

## Account types (fictional)
- ENTIN Savings: no monthly fee; interest paid monthly.
- ENTIN Current: for salaries and businesses; a monthly maintenance fee applies as shown in the app.

## Transfer limits (fictional ENTIN limits)
- Mobile app: up to 1,000,000 naira per day, raisable in-app with extra checks.
- USSD: up to 100,000 naira per day.
- These are demo values, not CBN limits.

## Things the assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot move money, reverse transactions, unlock profiles, reset PINs or passwords, or give investment advice.
