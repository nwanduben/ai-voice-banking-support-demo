# Security, Complaints and Escalation

## ENTIN Bank will NEVER ask for
Your PIN, OTP or one-time code, password or internet banking password, CVV (the 3 digits on the back of the card), card expiry date, token codes, or your full card number. We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account". Anyone who asks for these is a fraudster, even if they say they are from ENTIN or the CBN.

## If you shared a code or PIN
1. Tell us immediately so we can block your card.
2. Change your PIN and password in the app.
3. Report any transaction you don't recognise.

## Common scams in Nigeria
- Calls or SMS claiming to be from your bank or the CBN, asking you to "update your BVN" or read out a code.
- "Wrong transfer" scams: someone says they sent you money by mistake and pressures you to send it back.
- Fake credit alerts: always check your balance in the app.
- Fake POS agents or people offering to "help" at an ATM.

## Unauthorised or suspicious transactions
If you don't recognise a transaction, ENTIN will block the card, open a fraud case and connect you to the fraud team.

## Complaints and timelines
- Every complaint gets a reference that starts with T K T, and you receive SMS updates.
- Under the CBN Consumer Protection Regulations, banks must resolve complaints promptly. ENTIN aims to resolve most within 14 days (two weeks).
- If you're not satisfied with the outcome, you can escalate to the CBN Consumer Protection Department.
- Source: CBN Consumer Protection Regulations 2019: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/out/2019/ccd/cbn%20consumer%20protection%20regulations.pdf (verify the exact current timelines before public use).

## Speaking to a human
You can ask for a person at any time. High-risk issues (fraud, lost cards, restricted accounts, ATM disputes, failed identity checks) are always passed to a specialist with a summary, so you don't need to repeat yourself.

## Restricted accounts
If your account is restricted, a specialist will explain your options. The assistant can't discuss the reason.
